## THE TREACHERY OF GOLLUM CAMPAIGN

*Here we present you with The Treachery of Gollum campaign, a system for linking your games together in such a way that the results of one game can impact the next, or even one much later on.*

You can play this campaign with as few as two people, though, if you wish, different players can take control of different Scenarios - that way you can get a whole gaming group involved. However, it is important to note that you should play the Scenarios in order, as the results of one Scenario may change how a later one plays out.

**SCENARIO BONUSES**

After each Scenario has been completed, note down which side won that Scenario, either Good or Evil. This is important as, depending on who was victorious, there may be a bonus or penalty to a certain side in a future Scenario.

For example, if the Good side is victorious in 'The Paths of Cirith Ungol' Scenario, then the Good side will receive a bonus in the 'Shelob's Lair' Scenario - one much later in the campaign.

---

### (1) [THE PATHS OF CIRITH UNGOL](../scenarios/the_treachery_of_gollum.md#the-paths-of-cirith-ungol)

**Good Victory:** In the 'Shelob's Lair' Scenario, the Good player may reveal one of the tiles connected to the entrance tile before the game begins.

**Evil Victory:** In the 'Shelob's Lair' Scenario, the Evil player may deploy 6 Web Markers instead of 4.

### (2) [THE TAMING OF SMÉAGOL](../scenarios/the_treachery_of_gollum.md#the-taming-of-smeagol)

**Good Victory:** In 'The Dead Marshes' Scenario, the Good player may deploy Frodo, Sam and Sméagol within 6" of the centre of the western board edge.

**Evil Victory:** In 'The Dead Marshes' Scenario, the first time a Spectre becomes aware of the Good models and stops being a Sentry, the Evil player may immediately choose another Spectre which will also stop acting as a Sentry from that point onwards.

### (3) [THE DEAD MARSHES](../scenarios/the_treachery_of_gollum.md#the-dead-marshes)

**Good Victory:** In the 'Osgiliath' Scenario, Good reinforcements return to the battlefield on a 3+ rather than a 4+.

**Evil Victory:** In the 'Osgiliath' Scenario, Evil reinforcements return to the battlefield on a 3+ rather than a 4+.

### (4) [OSGILIATH](../scenarios/the_treachery_of_gollum.md#osgiliath)

**Good Victory:** In the 'Escape Through the Sewers' Scenario, when a Marker is revealed the Evil player does not roll a D6. Instead, the Good player may choose the result. They may not choose the same result twice.

**Evil Victory:** In the 'Escape Through the Sewers' Scenario, the first time a Marker is revealed, the Orcs that are placed do not act as Sentries. Instead they are automatically under the control of the Evil player.

### (5) [ESCAPE THROUGH THE SEWERS](../scenarios/the_treachery_of_gollum.md#escape-through-the-sewers)

**Good Victory:** In the 'Sméagol's Treachery' Scenario, Sam automatically has the first turn.

**Evil Victory:** In the 'Sméagol's Treachery' Scenario, Sméagol automatically has the first turn.

### (6) [SMÉAGOL'S TREACHERY](../scenarios/the_treachery_of_gollum.md#smeagols-treachery)

**Good Victory:** In the 'Shelob's Lair' Scenario, once during the game the Good player can have Frodo re-roll a failed Intelligence Test as part of the *"Hurry! This Way"* special rule.

**Evil Victory:** In the 'Shelob's Lair' Scenario, once during the game the Evil player can force Frodo to re-roll a successful Intelligence Test as part of the *"Hurry! This Way"* special rule.

### (7) [SHELOB'S LAIR](../scenarios/the_treachery_of_gollum.md#shelobs-lair)

**Good Victory:** In 'The Bravery of Master Samwise' Scenario, whilst Sam is within 3" of the webbed Frodo Marker he gains a bonus of +1 to his Duel Rolls.

**Evil Victory:** In 'The Bravery of Master Samwise' Scenario, if Shelob ever passes her Courage Test for her Survival Instinct special rule on the roll of a natural double, then the Wound is negated.

### (8) [THE BRAVERY OF MASTER SAMWISE](../scenarios/the_treachery_of_gollum.md#the-bravery-of-master-samwise)

**Good Victory:** In 'The End of All Things' Scenario, on the Crack of Doom board the Good player will always Activate Frodo and Sam before the Evil player chooses a power to use, regardless of which side has Priority.

**Evil Victory:** In 'The End of All Things' Scenario, the Evil player may use the Exhaustion power on a 2+ rather than a 3+.
