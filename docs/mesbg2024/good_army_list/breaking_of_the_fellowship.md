## ADDITIONAL RULES

- A Breaking of the Fellowship Army must include Aragorn, Strider who is always the Army's General.
- All Fellowship models must be included in Aragorn's Warband.
- Lothlórien Warriors can only be included in a Warband led by Haldir.

## SPECIAL RULES

**Gifts of Galadriel** - Each Fellowship model will gain the Wargear listed next to their name below, which have the effects detailed:

Frodo - Light of Earendil - ACTIVE - At the start of each of his Activations, Frodo may roll a D6. On a 3+, Frodo counts as having Cast the [Blinding Light] Magical Power.
Sam - Elven Rope - ACTIVE - Sam always counts as rolling a 6 for any Climb, Leap or Jump Tests he is required to make.
Merry & Pippin - Daggers of the Noldorin - ACTIVE - These are Elven hand weapons. Whilst wielding these weapons, Merry and Pippin may re-roll failed To Wound rolls when making Strikes.
Aragorn - Elven [Dagger] - ACTIVE - This is an Elven [hand weapon][Hand Weapon] with the Uruk-haibane special rule.
Legolas - [Bow] of the Galadhrim - ACTIVE - Whilst within 3" of a friendly Lothlórien model, Legolas may re-roll failed To Hit rolls when making shooting attacks.
Gimli - Galadriel's Locks - ACTIVE - Gimli may use this Wargear to re-roll a single D6 in a Duel Roll. Gimli may only use this Wargear three times during the course of the game.
Boromir - Golden Belt - ACTIVE - Boromir gains the [Woodland Creature] special rule.

**"You have my sword"** - So long as Frodo is alive and on the battlefield, friendly Fellowship models gain the [Fearless] special rule. Additionally, a Breaking of the Fellowship Army can never be considered Broken whilst Frodo is alive, even if he escapes the battlefield in Scenarios where this applies.

**Blessing of Galadriel** - Friendly models count as being under the effects of the [Fortify Spirit] Magical Power, even if they have no Will Points remaining.

**Shielded from Unfriendly Eyes** - Friendly Hero models gain an [Elven cloak][Elven Cloak] for free.
