## ADDITIONAL RULES

- Elf Warrior models can only be included in the Warband of an Elf Hero.
- Dwarf Warrior models can only be included in the Warband of a Dwarf Hero.
- Lake-town Warrior models can only be included in the Warband of a Lake-town Hero, or in the Warband of Gandalf the Grey or Bilbo Baggins.
- Eagle Warrior models can only be included in the Warband of an Eagle Hero, or in the Warband of Radagast the Brown or Beorn.
- The Battle of Five Armies may not include more Fledgeling Great Eagles than Great Eagles.

## SPECIAL RULES

**We stand together** - Friendly models may re-roll To Wound Rolls of a natural 1 when making Strikes if they are involved in a Combat with a friendly model that has a different racial Keyword to themselves.
