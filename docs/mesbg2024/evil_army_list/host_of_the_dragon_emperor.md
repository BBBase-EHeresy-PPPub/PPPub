## ADDITIONAL RULES

- A Host of the Dragon Emperor Army must always contain the Dragon Emperor of Rhûn, who is always the Army's General.

## SPECIAL RULES

**Heroes of the Easterlings** - Friendly Easterling Hero models may re-roll a single D6 in a Duel Roll.

**Easterling Phalanxes** - Friendly models Supported by two other friendly models gain a bonus of +1 To Wound when making Strikes.
