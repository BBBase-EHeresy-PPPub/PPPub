## ADDITIONAL RULES

- A Fell Beings of Mirkwood Army must always contain Razgûsh, who is always the Army's General.

## SPECIAL RULES

**Through the Forest** - Friendly Infantry models gain the [Woodland Creature] special rule.

**Death to the Elves** - Friendly models gain the [Hatred] (Elf) special rule.

**The War Leader** - Whilst Engaged in Combat with an enemy Elf model, Razgûsh gains a bonus of +1 to his Fight Value.
