## ARMY COMPOSITION

This army may include: Khandish King; Khandish Chieftain; Khandish Charioteer; Khandish Horseman; Khandish Warrior.

## ARMY BONUS

**"The Khandish Hosts"** - Khandish Horsemen and Khandish Charioteers do not count towards the force's Bow Limit.

Friendly Khandish models within 6" of a Khandish King count as being in range of a [banner].

- **Khandish Chariot**

| Mv | F | S | D | A | W | C |
|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|
| 10" | 0 | 3 | 7 | 0 | 3 | 3 |

**Defensive Bulwark:** Shooting hits the Chariot on an In The Way roll of 1-4 and the rider on a 5+. Strikes aimed at the rider also require a D6: on 1-4 they hit the Chariot, on 5+ the rider.

**Stable Platform:** The rider may fire a bow after moving the full distance.

**Chariot Charge:** The Chariot has [Monstrous Charge]. At the start of movement it may pivot up to 45 degrees, then move up to 10" straight ahead, pivoting up to 45 degrees after each full 3" moved. It ignores enemy Control Zones. Models contacted while moving, but not while pivoting, suffer two Strength 4 hits; Cavalry suffer two hits on both rider and mount. Might cannot modify these hits. If the target is slain, continue moving; if it survives, resolve the ensuing Combat with Monstrous Charge. A [Heroic March] does not prevent these hits. Instead of moving, the Chariot may pivot in any direction, unless moved by an enemy. It cannot jump physical barriers; when crossing a stream or gully, a roll of 1 throws the rider and destroys the Chariot. Models and terrain touched while pivoting are displaced only as far as needed.

**The Chariot and Magic:** Magical Powers may target the Chariot or its rider. Powers affecting multiple models affect both if in range. Only effects applied to the driver impede its movement. Powers cannot move the Chariot or knock it Prone, but still inflict their normal damage.
