## ARMY COMPOSITION

This army may include: The Balrog; Dragon; Durburz; Ashrak; Druzhag; Groblog; Moria Blackshield Captain; Moria Goblin Captain; Cave Drake; Moria Blackshield Shaman; Moria Goblin Shaman; The Watcher in the Water; Bat Swarm; Cave Troll; Dweller in the Dark; Giant Spider; Moria Blackshield; Moria Blackshield Drummers; Moria Goblin Drum; Moria Goblin Prowler; Moria Goblin Warrior; Warg Marauder; Wild Warg.

## ARMY BONUS

**"We cannot get out, they are coming"** - Friendly Moria Goblin models involved in a Fight where their opponent is Trapped receive a bonus of +1 to their Fight value.

### THE WATCHER IN THE WATER [200 POINTS]

**KRAKEN, MONSTER, INFANTRY, HERO - INDEPENDENT HERO**

| Mv | F | S | D | A | W | C | M | W | F |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|

**WARGEAR**

- Tentacles

**HEROIC ACTIONS**

- [Heroic Strength]

**SPECIAL RULES**

- [Harbinger of Evil]
- [Resistant to Magic]
- [Terror]

- **From the Deep** `ACTIVE`

Do not deploy the Watcher with the army; it cannot be part of another Hero's Warband. In each Priority Phase after Priority is rolled, you may declare that it will arrive and roll a D6. On a 3+, immediately place it anywhere on the battlefield. After declaring its arrival, you must roll in every subsequent Priority Phase until it arrives. Displace any models the minimum distance needed to leave them 1" away, or as close as possible; players alternate displacing models, starting with the Watcher's player. The Watcher cannot Charge on the turn it arrives.

- **Many Tentacles** `PASSIVE`

At 3 Wounds or less, The Watcher's Attack value becomes 3.

- **Tentacles** `ACTIVE`

The Watcher can make D6 shooting attacks, range 6" and S3, with no In The Way tests. Models hit are dragged into base contact.

- **Water Dweller** `PASSIVE`

Whilst within water, The Watcher's Move distance is doubled, and gains the [Monstrous Charge] special rule.

- **Gaping Maw** `BRUTAL POWER ATTACK`

Whilst within water, The Watcher can choose a single Man-sized (or smaller) model and instantly slay them unless saved with Fate. The Watcher is removed from the board and needs to re-arrive as per the From the Deep special rule.
